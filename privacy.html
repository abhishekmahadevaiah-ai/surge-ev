SURGE / Site policy

Privacy policy

This notice explains what information SURGE handles when you visit this website or contact the team about charging infrastructure.

Effective date / 11 September 2026

Information we receive

This static website does not require an account and does not collect payment details. If you email Surge Electric Pvt Ltd or use the contact form, the information you choose to send is handled by your email provider and by the SURGE team to respond to your request.

Preferences and analytics

The site stores your cookie preference in browser storage so the consent message does not appear on every visit. Optional analytics are disabled by default and load only after you choose “Allow analytics”. When enabled, the site uses Google Analytics 4 with IP anonymisation. You can reopen Privacy settings at any time to withdraw or change your choice.

Hosting and security

The site is delivered through Cloudflare Pages over HTTPS. Cloudflare may process limited technical request data as part of delivering and securing the service. SURGE does not place secrets, payment credentials, or private API keys in this website’s frontend.

How we use information

  • To reply to partnership, support, or station-hosting enquiries.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the website.
  • To understand anonymous website usage when optional analytics are enabled.

Your choices

You can choose necessary storage only, allow analytics, or reopen Privacy settings later using the control at the bottom of the page. Withdrawing analytics removes the site’s accessible Google Analytics cookies and prevents the tag from loading again. Contact enquiries are sent to the SURGE Google Form and linked response Sheet only after the form is configured. For direct questions, email contact desk. You can also clear site data in your browser settings.
