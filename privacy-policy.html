Privacy Policy

This draft explains how information is handled when you visit surgecharging.com or contact SURGE. It is written for an Indian business and requires review against the actual operating model and applicable requirements before publication.

Draft for review · Legal review required before production launch.

Draft prepared 27 September 2026 · Effective date to be confirmed

Who operates this website

The website is operated by SURGE Energy Pvt. Ltd. (“SURGE”, “we”, “us”). The company name was supplied for this draft and has not been independently checked against corporate registration records. No registered office address was supplied, so none is published here.

Contact: support@surgecharging.com · +91 9110605621 · surgecharging.com.

Information we may receive

  • Contact enquiries: name, email address, selected enquiry topic and message that you choose to provide. Do not include passwords, payment credentials, government identity numbers or sensitive personal information.
  • Technical request data: hosting and security providers may receive IP address, browser/device information, requested pages and timestamps when they serve the website.
  • Consent preference: this site stores the choice “necessary only” or “analytics” in browser local storage. It is a browser storage item, not a cookie.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics is configured to load only after you affirmatively choose analytics. If enabled, it may process page views, event data, device/browser details and identifiers under Google’s configuration.

Why and how information is used

  • To understand and respond to support, fleet, charging or site-hosting enquiries.
  • To operate, protect and troubleshoot the website and to prevent abuse.
  • To understand aggregate website activity only if you choose optional analytics.

Marketing communications are not requested by the current website form. If SURGE adds marketing, it will describe the purpose and provide a separate, optional choice where appropriate.

Where information is stored and who may access it

The website is hosted through Cloudflare Pages. The current contact form is a user-initiated email handoff: the browser prepares a message for your email application, and information is sent only if you review and send that email. SURGE cannot confirm delivery from this website.

The repository contains a Google Forms adapter, but its form endpoint is not configured for the current contact page. If a Google Form, response Sheet, CRM, charging platform, payment processor or other processor is enabled later, the notice, access controls, storage region, contractual terms and retention details must be verified and this page updated before use.

Access to enquiries should be restricted to personnel who need them to provide support. SURGE must confirm the actual mailbox access list and retention controls before launch.

Retention

Enquiry details should be kept only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage the resulting relationship, address disputes and meet applicable obligations. A specific deletion schedule and operational process have not been supplied; SURGE must define and implement them before production launch. Browser consent preference remains until you change or clear it.

Payments and charging services

This static website does not collect payment card or bank details and does not process a charging-session payment. If charging or payment services are integrated through a separate app, charging platform or payment provider, those services may process transaction, vehicle/session or payment information under the notices shown in that service. Provider, data fields, storage, retention and support process must be confirmed before integration.

Service providers and external links

Website delivery, typography, decorative imagery, motion and opt-in analytics use third-party providers (currently Cloudflare, Google Fonts, Pexels for the illustrative homepage photograph, Anime.js via esm.sh and Google Analytics when enabled). The photograph is a stock image and does not depict a verified SURGE charging site; its photographer credit appears over the image. The homepage requests the photograph and animation library even if analytics is declined. Those requests send technical connection information to their providers; the site does not intentionally send enquiry data or analytics events in those requests. Tabler Icons are served locally. Clicking the Google Maps directions link takes you to Google; the map is not automatically embedded on page load. Each provider may receive technical request data and processes it under its own terms and privacy notice. The provider list must be rechecked whenever integrations change.

Security

The static site is configured for HTTPS and uses security response headers in the deployment configuration. No internet service can be represented as risk-free. Contact data security, mailbox access, incident handling and final production headers require operational verification; the static contact flow has no server-side form endpoint.

Your choices and data requests

You can decline analytics, change or withdraw that choice using the “Privacy settings” control, and clear site storage through your browser. If you want to ask about, access, correct or request deletion of information you sent to SURGE, or raise a privacy concern, contact support@surgecharging.com or +91 9110605621. Requests will be handled under applicable requirements. This draft does not assert that a particular statutory regime or certification has been verified.

Updates and review

We may revise this notice when the site or its data practices change. The final policy, applicable Indian privacy/data-protection requirements, grievance workflow, legal identity, retention periods and third-party processing arrangements must be reviewed by a qualified Indian legal professional and the SURGE service owner before launch.
